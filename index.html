Risica is Chef Andrea Frizzi's pizzeria and wine bar, inside the Edit River North building in RiNo.

The pizzas are built on San Marzano tomato, fior di latte and Grana Padano, with seasonal ones like acorn squash with Sottocenere and hot honey. Around them are small plates for the table: sausage meatballs, beef carpaccio, burrata with rosemary focaccia, oysters by the piece.

Order a few things, share everything, and pick something from a list of 32 Italian wines to go with it. We're open for lunch and dinner six days a week.

The chef

Andrea Frizzi

Andrea Frizzi is the chef behind Denver's Il Posto and Vero. Risica is his take on the neighborhood pizzerias and wine bars of Milan, where dinner can be a pizza, a plate of salumi and a good bottle.

“Chi non risica non rosica.”

“Who doesn’t risk, doesn’t get a bite.” Nothing ventured, nothing gained. — Andrea Frizzi

It’s an old Italian proverb, and it’s where the name Risica comes from.
